# Scientific and operational limitations

- Downstream deposited-score evaluation; no native inference, training or clinical validation.
- 20/25 primary comparisons reproduce; five stored CCTop reference discrepancies persist.
- TrueOT is externally sourced and filtered for known overlap, but complete model-training independence remains unverified.
- Sequence/chromosome identity can merge loci/context; 13 primary identities have multiple starts. Length-23 is a no-bulge proxy.
- Scored-cohort selection, seeded candidate universes, nomination bias, assay detection limits and heterogeneous labels constrain interpretation.
- Small guide/study counts and incomplete quantitative external outcomes limit transportability. Some strata are single-class.
- Bootstrap intervals depend on the dataset/resampling unit. Paired recall uncertainty includes zero; no superiority follows.
- Coordinate checks do not verify sequences against a reference genome.
- Short scalability runs retain duplicates/dependence and unflushed caches. Long-term memory leaks, concurrency and other platforms remain unverified.
- Original source/fixtures are MIT. External truth/TrueOT and derived-data rights remain unresolved and are excluded from release.
- No methodological novelty or improved biological prediction accuracy is established.
